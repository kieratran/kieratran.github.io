NASA Student Airborne Research Program (2026)

I was a an intern at NASA as a graduate mentor for terrestrial ecology (TECo) group in the Student Airborne Research Program, working with Dr. Batuhan Osmanoglu on land processes and vegetation responses using synthetic aperture radar (SAR). We spent the first 2 weeks doing airborne surveys and ground-truthing trips; and the other 6 weeks at Virginia Commonwealth University to do research. Check out my student's awesome work through their presentations.

  • Charlie Rosemond: Temporal Wildfire Burn Severity Analysis with Airborne and Spaceborne L-Band SAR
  • Ella Boussy: Analyzing Biomass in Protected and Unprotected Areas Around the Chesapeake Bay Using GEDI Data
  • Catherine Pope: Remote Sensing Vegetation Response to Repeated Glacial Lake Outburst Floods in the Mendenhall Wetlands, Alaska
  • Jackson Retelle: Analyzing the Efficacy of SAR for Landslide Detection in Western North Carolina
  • Morgan Fellows: Using SAR to identify tornado tracks from the March 14th, 2025 Midwestern US outbreak
  • Alissa Ferguson: Sentinel-1A InSAR GNSS Framework for Temporal Monitoring of Kīlauea with Applications to NISAR
