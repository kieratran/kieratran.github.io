Main Projects

Radar-derived basal melt rates of Ross Ice Shelf, Antractica

This project examines the relationship between airborne ice-penetrating radar attenuation rates and ice-shelf basal melt rates through ice temperature at Ross Ice Shelf (RIS). We build a 1.5D thermal model to capture this direct relationship and convert melt rates from depth-resolved multiple-reflector attenuation rates. Radar data was from [ROSETTA-Ice airborne survey](https://pgg.ldeo.columbia.edu/projects/rosetta-ice). We discover higher basal melt rates at grounding zones of both eastern and western RIS, which reveal complex mixtures of melting processes caused by tides, ocean currents, subglacial hydrology discharge, and basal topography. This work is currently in prep.
